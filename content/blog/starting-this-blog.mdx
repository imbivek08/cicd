## Why I am writing

This blog is where I will share practical notes from building projects, learning DevOps, and solving problems in real applications.

The goal is simple: publish one useful idea every week. Posts may cover deployment, backend architecture, frontend decisions, Linux, or lessons learned from a mistake.

## What to expect

- Short, practical explanations
- Commands and examples that can be reused
- Honest notes about what worked and what did not

The next post will focus on a specific problem and the solution I used.
